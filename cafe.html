For the grown-ups

We're here when mom needs a time out.

The café at Little Beans is a place to give the grown-ups a break. Meet a friend and have a real conversation. Keep an eye on the little ones in the play space while you chat, get some work done, or just exist quietly for a minute.

BYO always welcome
High-speed WiFi
Adults always free
BRING YOUR OWN

Outside food & drinks are always welcome.

No asterisks, no exceptions. Grab a latte on the way. Pack your lunch. Feed the kids at home and bring yourself something to actually eat — with both hands — without someone tugging at your leg or trying to spill your salad. We just want you to be comfortable.

THE SPACE

Clean tables, a good view, and no rush.

Plenty of room to spread out, and close to the play space so you're never out of the loop. Bring your laptop, bring your book, bring your lunch — this is your time too.

AT THE COUNTER

Snacks, drinks & coffee.

For the kids and the grown-ups — a little something for everyone at the front counter.

COME AS YOU ARE

Your table is waiting.

Walk in any time during open hours — no reservation needed. The kids will find their people, and so will you.
